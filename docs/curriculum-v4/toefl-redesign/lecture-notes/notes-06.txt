写作考试概述
写作分题型介绍
Build a Sentence
In the Build a Sentence task, test takers see one intact sentence followed by another sentence with words
or phrases in the wrong order. The two sentences form an exchange between speakers. Test takers
move the words and phrases to form a grammatical sentence that is an appropriate reply in the context.
This
task measures the test taker’s command of sentence structures, a skill that is essential for
communication.

Sample High-Level Response

Write an Email
In the Write an Email task, test takers are presented with a scenario in text regarding either an academic
or social setting. Test takers are asked to share information in writing for a specific communicative
purpose--for example, making a recommendation, extending an invitation, or proposing a solution to a
problem. Test takers have 7 minutes to complete this task. This writing task measures the test taker’s
ability to produce a multisentence written text that
• achieves the designated communicative goal, following basic social conventions;
• is adequately elaborated, clear, and cohesive;
• makes accurate and appropriate use of a range of grammatical structures and vocabulary; and
• follows the mechanical conventions of English (spelling, punctuation, and capitalization).

A fully successful response
The response is effective, is clearly expressed, and shows consistent facility in the use of language.
A typical response displays the following:
• Elaboration that effectively supports the communicative purpose
• Effective syntactic variety and precise, idiomatic word choice
• Consistent use of appropriate social conventions (e.g., politeness, register, organization of
information and formulation of actions such as requests, refusals, criticisms, etc.)
• Almost no lexical or grammatical errors other than those expected from a competent writer writing
under timed conditions (e.g., common typos or common misspellings or substitutions like there/their)
Write for an Academic Discussion
In the Write for an Academic Discussion task, test takers are asked to state and support an opinion within
the context of an online class discussion forum. A post from the professor briefly frames the topic and
poses an opinion question related to the topic for the class to discuss. Brief posts from two students then
provide different positions on the issue. The test takers contribute their own position on the question,
supporting their opinion with their own reasoning, experiences, or knowledge. They have 10 minutes to
complete this task. This task measures the test taker’s ability to produce a multisentence written text that
• clearly elaborates an argument for a position, responding to arguments, and/or using information
provided in short texts;
• is adequately supported, clear, and cohesive;
• makes accurate and appropriate use of a range of grammatical structures and vocabulary; and
• follows the mechanical conventions of English (spelling, punctuation, and capitalization).

造句题解题技巧
一、分析句子结构，确定核心成分
找主语：先确定句子的主语（人或物），比如题目中是否明确出现 “He” “It” “I” “My friend Jenna” 等明确的
动作执行者。
找谓语动词：识别核心动词或动词短语，如 “wanted to know” “was moved” “am curious” 等，明确句子的
动作。
找从句引导词：像 “when” “if” “where” 等引导词，往往提示从句结构，帮助梳理句子的逻辑层次，比如 “when
it ended” “if he mentioned” 等从句，是对前面内容的补充说明
二、结合疑问词和语境，梳理逻辑关系
题目通常以疑问句形式提出，要结合问题的语境来组织答案。例如问题 “What did Andre ask you at the
party?”（安德烈在派对上问你什么了？），就要围绕 “问的内容” 来构建包含 “wanted to know” 的句子，
逻辑上是 “他想知道某事”。
注意时间、条件等逻辑线索，比如 “rescheduled”（重新安排）对应 “was moved to a time when...”（被改
到…… 的时间），体现因果和时间逻辑。
三、关注固定搭配和语法规则
固定短语：如 “want to know” “be curious if” “wonder when” 等是常见的固定表达，要熟练掌握这些搭配，
快速组合核心结构。
时态一致：根据问题的时态（一般过去时、现在时等）调整句子动词的时态，比如问题用一般过去时，回
答中的动词也要相应使用过去式（如 “wanted” “was moved”）。
代词指代：注意 “it” “he” “she” 等代词的指代对象，确保指代清晰、逻辑连贯，比如 “it” 指代前文的 “the
meeting” “the new restaurant” 等。

Using Classification to Support Ideas
为了能将解释、例子的内容更具体和有条理的展开，同学们可以对题目中的名词性内容或概念进行逻辑分
类（classification）。这种拆分可以帮助同学们避免关键词过于笼统，同时也可以拓展思路来源。常见的分
类方向如下：
对象年龄
e.g. people → young / middle-age / elderly people
使用场景
e.g. policy → education / healthcare / housing
规模大小
e.g. organizations → small / medium-sized / large organizations
对象职业
e.g. citizen → unemployed / employed / retired citizens
分情况讨论注意事项
• 分类角度：一分为二/三，没有交集
• 分类结果：可以一边倒，也可以平衡结构
