First, it's because they're healthier for our bodies. For example, if we eat too much
meat-based food, like burgers or pizza, we consume a lot of fat but not enough fiber and
vitamins. With a plant-based diet, people would likely become healthier overall.
Second, I think such a diet is more environmentally friendly. If people continue to hunt
animals and raise them just for meat, the ecosystem could be seriously damaged. Take
cattle farming for example—it uses huge amounts of water and land, and it also produces a
lot of greenhouse gases.

Sample Answer
Personally, I agree with this idea. From a health perspective, eating at consistent times
helps maintain a healthy digestive system. For example, when I'm too busy with work and
skip lunch, I often end up feeling extremely hungry later. This usually leads me to crave
high-calorie foods like burgers or pizza, which are not very good for my stomach, especially
if I eat them late at night.
From a financial standpoint, sticking to a regular meal schedule can also help save money. If
you miss a meal, you often end up ordering takeout, which is usually much more expensive
than eating at home or bringing your own food.
